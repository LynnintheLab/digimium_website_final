Retrieved: **2026-11-15**. Scope: proposed UX wireframes only. The owner confirmed the five accepted payment names; these image files do not implement payment processing or establish a provider partnership.

Original artwork is stored unchanged. Keep its proportions (`object-fit: contain`), original colors and background. All five files are square and below 50 KB. The JPG app icons are opaque, not transparent. Provider trademarks/copyright remain with their respective owners; no general redistribution or endorsement license is claimed.

KBZ and AYA are represented by **KBZPay** and **AYA Pay** wallet icons to match the owner's payment reference. They should not be relabelled as corporate bank-transfer logos. Before implementing actual payment instructions, confirm the exact receiving service and account details separately.

## Retrieval notes

The public Wave Money and AYA Pay homepages returned HTTP 403 to direct HTTP retrieval. No login, CAPTCHA bypass, browser security override or credentials were used. The consumer app icons came from Apple's public artwork URLs returned by the exact App Store records; seller names and product identities were checked against the provider pages. The KBZPay footer image labelled “KBZPay Logo” was visually a chat-support icon and was rejected. The Binance social-sharing card was also rejected in favor of its published app-style mark.
