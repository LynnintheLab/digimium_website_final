# Digimium
<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->
## Platform
web
## Product purpose
A bilingual website introducing Digimium's premium digital subscription collection to Myanmar-speaking students, creators, and professionals. Existing brand materials establish AI, creative, music, entertainment, and everyday software categories.
## Brand commitments
User requests a clean, minimalist modern website with brand-blue logos and soft blue/lavender gradient backgrounds instead of a white canvas. Wa Lone for Myanmar. Original supplied logos preserved. English uses self-hosted Manrope for simple, modern headings, body copy, and controls; the user requested replacing the supplied branding font.
## Capabilities and constraints
Browse and search the supplied catalog. An account-restricted admin panel manages service names, descriptions, categories, optional prices/durations, availability, featured visibility, and contact settings. Catalog edits persist in D1 and propagate to server-rendered storefront pages. Contact details are from supplied brand tokens. Show only the prices and plans approved for publication. Customers order by choosing a plan, adding it to a cart or checking out directly to the store’s Telegram DM. The customer sends the final message. Payment processing is deferred. Do not invent prices, availability or activation times.
## Evidence
Branding data and catalog supplied in the parent design-media workspace. Assets copied locally for portability. No testimonials or numerical business claims are introduced.
