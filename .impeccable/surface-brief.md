# Homepage
Mode: Persuade. Browse a digital service, understand how to enquire, contact Digimium.
## Direction contract
THESIS: A calm, bilingual digital collection. Typographic confidence and the actual Digimium portal mark make the identity; no generic software dashboard.
OWN-WORLD: Soft blue/lavender gradient canvas, deep blue display panel, Persian Blue actions, simple modern Manrope and generous Wa Lone line-height, fine neutral dividers.
STORY: Understand the collection, explore services by purpose, select a service, enquire about current plans.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Quiet horizontal navigation, oversized left-aligned English headline with one blue line, Myanmar supporting copy and two actions. Right side is a dark graphic product stage with the real brand symbol and three changeable service collections. A narrow category line grounds the composition.
FORM: Reference-led minimalist bilingual storefront; user-requested blue gradients and colored logos evolve the visual world. No randomized concept selection.
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

## Admin surface
Mode: Operate. A persistent service catalog editor with a left service list, focused form, explicit save state, and contact settings. Single-snapshot saves use revision checks to protect concurrent edits. Blue-tinted panels and Manrope maintain continuity.
